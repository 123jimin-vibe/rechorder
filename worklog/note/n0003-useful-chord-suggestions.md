## Principles

- Research and future requirements only; suggestions are not implemented or authorized by this note.
- Suggestions should explain musical relationships and respect user intent. Scale membership alone is insufficient; rules depend on musical style.

## Required foundations

1. Harmonic context: explicit or uncertain key/tonal center, mode, local key changes and Roman-numeral interpretation.
2. Progression relationships: preceding/following chords, harmonic function, cadences, applied dominants and borrowed chords.
3. Voice-leading evaluation: common tones, leaps, bass movement, tendency-tone resolution and register, with concrete candidate voicings.
4. Intent and constraints: distinguish next chords, replacements and alternative voicings; respect supplied melody/bass and chosen style; explain each suggestion.

For Dm7 → G7 → ? in C major, Cmaj7 may be explained as dominant-to-tonic resolution, with a voicing retaining G and moving B to C and F to E. Timing is needed for harmonic rhythm, passing notes and suspensions. Do not infer a pedal or passing bass from an isolated chord.
